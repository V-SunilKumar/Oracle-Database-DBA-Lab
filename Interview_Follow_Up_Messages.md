# Interview Follow-Up Messages

A collection of message templates for job applications, recruiter outreach, interview follow-ups, and thank-you notes.

## 1. Main Message — When You Applied to a Role

Hi {{Name}}, thanks for connecting.

I’m reaching out regarding the {{Job Title}} role at {{Company}}. After reviewing the position, it aligns well with my background in {{your field / key skills}}.

I applied earlier today and wanted to introduce myself directly.

Would you be the right person to speak with about this role, or is there someone you’d recommend I connect with?

Thanks in advance, I appreciate your time.

### Follow-Up #1 — After 1 Business Day

Hi {{Name}}, just following up on my previous message regarding the role. I remain very interested and would appreciate any guidance you can share. Thank you!

### Follow-Up #2 — After 3 Business Days

Hi {{Name}}, I know schedules get busy, so I wanted to gently follow up. I’m very enthusiastic about the opportunity and would value any direction you can provide.

### Follow-Up #3 — Final Check-In After 4 Business Days

Hi {{Name}}, I’ll make this my last follow-up. I remain very interested and would appreciate any advice on next steps or who is best to connect with. Thanks again for your time.

---

## 2. Main Message — When There Is No Open Role

I’m reaching out because I’m interested in {{Company}} and wanted to ask directly: are you planning to open any {{relevant roles}} roles in the near future, or do you already have internal coverage for this area?

Thanks for your time, and I’m happy to share more context or my CV if helpful.

Best regards,  
{{Your Name}}

## 3. Follow-Up Template After First-Round Interview

Hi PersonName,

I hope you’re doing well! I just wanted to follow up on my interview with ContactName on DATE for the TITLE role.

I enjoyed our conversation, the way he/she explained the company and the role made me really excited about the opportunity.

He/she mentioned I’d hear back in about two weeks, so I thought I’d check in to see if there are any updates on my application.

Thank you for your time in advance.

Best,  
Name

## 4. Thank-You Template After an Interview

**Subject:** Thank You - [Job Title] Interview

Hi name,

It was such a pleasure speaking with you earlier today, thank you again for taking the time. I enjoyed our discussion about [specific topic you connected on], and it gave me an even clearer sense of the impact this role has on the team.

I left our conversation feeling even more excited about the opportunity at [Company Name], and I’d love the chance to bring [specific strength/skill you mentioned] to the team.

Looking forward to what’s ahead, and please let me know if there’s anything else you need from me in the meantime.

All the best,

your name
